TRANSFORMERS: WAR FOR CYBERTRON - VR MOD (early version)
========================================================

(Русская версия: "VR-мод — инструкция.txt")

TWO MODES
  "Launch VR.bat" ............. MAIN: first person, aim with the right controller,
                                crosshair in the headset, sharp square render, menus on a screen.
  "Launch VR (classic).bat" ... the saved older version: third person, aim with your gaze.
  ("Запуск VR.bat" and "Запуск VR (классика).bat" are the same launchers with Russian names.)

HOW TO START
1. Start Virtual Desktop (the Streamer on the PC + the app in the headset) and connect.
2. Run the .bat you want from the game folder.
3. Menus, the pause screen and movies are shown on a big virtual screen.
   Gameplay switches to full 3D with head tracking.

The normal non-VR game still starts from Binaries\TWFC.exe (with your ReShade/DLSS).
The mod lives in the BinariesVR and BinariesVR_Classic folders and changes nothing in Binaries.
The VR resolution (1800x1800) is used only by the VR version; the normal game's resolution
setting in the registry is not changed.

LANGUAGE
  The mod's VR settings menu and weapon wheel are in English or Russian. By default the language follows
  Windows (Russian, Ukrainian or Belarusian Windows - Russian, anything else - English).
  Switch it in the headset: the first item of the VR settings menu, "Language / Язык".
  Or in BinariesVR\twfcvr.ini, section [General]: Language=auto / en / ru.
  (The game's own menus and subtitles stay in the game's language.)

CONTROLS (main mode; the buttons work like an Xbox gamepad)
  Right controller ................ AIM: the weapon fires where the controller points,
                                    the ring crosshair shows the direction
  Left stick ...................... move
  Right stick ..................... turn (smooth; snap turn - SnapTurnDegrees=30 in the ini)
  Right trigger ................... fire (with a melee weapon in the right hand: swing it)
  Left trigger .................... as in the game: precise aim / the weapon's second action (detonating grenades);
                                    in vehicle form - boost
  Y ............................... main ability (RB), at once, held while you hold Y
  Right stick UP (hold) ........... second ability (LB), held while you hold the stick up (the right stick click
                                    does the same). Turning is sideways only: up/down do not turn.
  B: short press .................. the game's B: throws a grenade (and whatever else the game has on B);
                                    grenades also from the belt with the grip
  B: hold 0.35 s .................. the right hand's WEAPON WHEEL (like Half-Life: Alyx): point the hand at an item
                                    and release B. Items: your weapons / "Empty hand". (The grips do not open the
                                    wheel - they only grab.) In a vehicle, holding B switches the vehicle's weapon.
  Right stick DOWN (flick) ........ next weapon; with an empty right hand - the gun back into it.
                                    In a vehicle - the vehicle's next weapon.
  Left stick click ................ transform.
                                    Empty right hand: the gun is put away and does not fire, the hand is free
                                    (grenades from the belt, pulling, fingers); its position on the controller
                                    is its own, set in "Weapon setup" while the hand is empty (EmptyGripR)
  Melee ........................... a sharp punch FORWARD (where you look) with the left or the empty right hand -
                                    the game's attack straight ahead (no melee weapon needed)
  Left grip at the front of the gun  two-handed grip (the back of the gun follows the right controller,
                                    the barrel turns to the left hand)
  A - jump, X - reload / interact
  Grip (free hand, not at the belt) . use: pick up, levers, buttons (GripUse=1 in [Input])
  "Empty hand" in the wheel on a turret: get off the turret. On a turret: aim with both hands
                                    (between them), the eyes are raised by TurretEyeUp=0.35 m ([VR]); the
                                    triggers work as with a gun: right - fire, left - aim (also with both
                                    hands on the grips). The shoulder pads are hidden on a turret (the view)
  (in a vehicle: Y - the vehicle's special move)
  The old layout (grips = abilities, Y = next weapon): GripAbilities=1 in [Input].
  (in the old layout the stick clicks are LS / RS)
  Menu button (left): short press .. Start (pause)
                      hold + press A (or hold 1.5 s) in gameplay ... SETUP MODE (the game gets no buttons
                      meanwhile; a floating panel with the VR SETTINGS MENU appears in front of you):
      - pick items with the right hand's ray + trigger (on a value: the left half lowers it, the right half
        raises it), or with the left stick (up/down - item, left/right - value) and A; the right stick scrolls.
        Changes apply at once;
      - "Language / Язык" - English / Russian;
      - "Vehicle camera position" (vehicle form only) - like Space Mover: hold a grip and drag the world with
        your hand (forward/back, up/down), right trigger - save, B - cancel. The cockpit or the chase camera,
        whichever is selected;
      - "Vehicle: third-person view" - switches the vehicle view;
      - "Weapon setup" (or Y) - the weapon page (Y - back to the menu). The hands work by role: the gun hand
        and the other one;
      - the gun hand's grip: hold it - the gun freezes; move your hand to where it is comfortable to hold
        the gun, release (only the position changes; B - change the rotation too). The orange square is where
        your hand is now. For a gun in the left hand each weapon has its own grip (GunGripL), not a mirror
        of the right one;
      - the green squares are zones: at the front of the gun (two-handed grip) and on the belt (grenades).
        Grab a square with the other hand's grip and move it;
      - A - reset this weapon's settings;
      - press the menu button again (or the "Save and exit" item) - leave (it also leaves by itself after
        3 minutes). Every change is saved at once. The grip and foregrip are stored per weapon and per
        character ([Weapon.<name>@<character>] sections in twfcvr.ini; a character not set up for a weapon
        yet takes that weapon's last setup - [Weapon.<name>]). Aiming is only along the barrel (the extra scope lens is removed; Scope=1 in [Input]
        brings it back).
  The game's interface (health, ammo, objectives) hangs on a panel in front of you and smoothly
  follows your gaze. The game's own crosshair in the centre is removed - aim with the ring.

  twfcvr.ini can be edited while the game runs - changes apply within a second
  (except RenderWidth/RenderHeight). Handy for tuning WorldScale (the size of the world:
  bigger = you are bigger) and FirstPersonEyeHeight (eye height), and in [HUD] - the Distance,
  Width and FollowSpeed of the interface panel.

SETTINGS - BinariesVR\twfcvr.ini (open with Notepad)
  RenderWidth/RenderHeight=1800   render resolution of each eye in VR.
                      Higher - sharper, but more GPU load (both eyes are drawn in one frame).
  CameraMode=1        1 = first person, 0 = third person
  AimMode=1           1 = aim with the controller, 0 = with your gaze
  Reticle=1           crosshair; ReticleDistance=8 - how far (m)
  FirstPersonEyeHeight=150   eye height in first person
  WorldScale=200      head movement scale: bigger = you are "bigger", stronger depth
  Mode=1              1 = 3D in gameplay, 0 = always the virtual screen
  FpsLimit=120 / VRFpsLimit=300   FPS limit without / with the headset
  ScreenDistance / ScreenWidth - size and distance of the virtual screen (metres)
  Language=auto       [General]: the mod's language - auto, en or ru

TIPS
- Both eyes are drawn from the very same game instant (StereoPerFrame=1 by default): the vehicle in
  third person, robots, hands and effects are always solid and 3D, with no "swimming" in motion.
  If the GPU cannot keep up in heavy scenes, lower RenderWidth/RenderHeight (e.g. 1500-1600), the refresh
  rate in Virtual Desktop (72/80 Hz), or go back to alternating eyes (StereoPerFrame=0 in [VR]).
- To turn "forward" - the usual Virtual Desktop / Oculus recentre.

HANDS (new, early version)
  The robot's arms and weapon follow the controllers (HandIK=1 in the ini, 0 = off).
  Your own robot's head is hidden so it does not block the view.
  Robots near you no longer vanish (NoNearFade=600 - the radius; 0 = as in the game).
  ShowBody=1 - arms and legs visible, the bulky torso hidden (0 = arms only, 2 = the whole body).
  ArmSize=0.8 - thickness of the whole arms: forearms, armour, the arm on the empty hand (1 = as in the game).
    In game: VR settings menu -> "Arm size".
  LimbThickness=0.5 - the upper arms (shoulder to elbow) thinner still, on top of ArmSize,
    LegThickness=0.75 - thinner legs (1 = as in the game).
  HandScale=1.0 / WeaponScale=1.0 - size of the robot's hands and of the weapon in hand (1 = as in the game).
  HandOffsetForward / HandOffsetRight / HandOffsetUp - shift the robot's hands relative to yours, in cm
    (e.g. the robot's hands are right of yours - HandOffsetRight=-4). Can be changed in game.
  MuzzleFlashScale=0.35 - size of muzzle flashes (they are right at your eyes); also for turrets - mounted
    and carried.
  The aim ring sits where the shot will really land (on a wall, an enemy), not at a fixed distance.
  StereoPerFrame=1 - both eyes are drawn in one engine tick from one state of the world and physics (a moving
    vehicle, robots and hands are exactly in sync in both eyes; 0 - the old alternating eyes every other frame).
  SingleThreadRender=1 - the game draws on its main thread, so every image goes to its own eye with its own
    pose (does not slip after menus, movies, loads). 0 - as before (a separate render thread: slightly more
    FPS, but the eyes can slip).
  NearClip=10 - how close to the eyes things are still visible (the game had 100 - hands vanished at the face).
  The gun is as in the game: the forearm turns into the gun from the elbow, it points exactly along the right
    controller and the elbow follows it; the hand is a fist on its grip. An empty hand is a full-size fist on a
    thick forearm.
  In a vehicle you can aim with your gaze (VehicleHeadAim=1; 0 = with the controller).
  Vehicle camera: VehicleCamera=0 - from the cockpit, 1 - chase camera behind and above (ChaseDistance /
    ChaseHeight).

PHYSICAL CONTROLS (new)
  Melee - only when a melee weapon is picked in the wheel: a sharp swing of the hand holding it.
    A hit counts only when the weapon itself reaches another robot during the swing (strong vibration) -
    then the game strikes exactly that robot. Swinging at the air does nothing: no animation, no lunge, you
    walk freely. Swing threshold - MeleeSpeed=3.0 (m/s), weapon length - MeleeReach=0.55 (m), robot
    "thickness" - MeleeHitRadius=90 ([Input] section); MeleeContact=0 - as before (any swing = a hit).
    The trigger with a melee weapon in the right hand no longer strikes (only the swing does).
  Two-handed grip - bring the left hand to the front of the gun (the grip point comes from the weapon model)
    and squeeze the left grip: the robot's left hand goes onto the gun, the barrel turns so the foregrip is in
    your left hand.
  PhysicalAim=1 - the two-handed grip with the gun at your eyes also presses the left trigger (off by default:
    on some guns the left trigger is the second action, e.g. detonating grenades).
  Melee weapon: pick "Melee" in the weapon wheel - the robot's melee weapon (Megatron's hammer) stays in
    the hand, strike by swinging (or with the right trigger if it is in the right hand). Put it away - pick
    a gun in the wheel or briefly squeeze the right grip. In the left hand: a gun in the right, the melee
    weapon in the left at the same time. Transforming puts it away.
  Abilities (B, right stick up) - for AbilityAnimation=1.2 s the arms are given to the game's animation (spins,
    special attacks), then smoothly return to the controllers.
  BodyFollowHead=1 - the body stays under your head when you walk around the room.
  MoveByHead=1 ([Input] section) - the left stick moves where your head looks.
  The vehicle drives and fires where the right controller points (VehicleHeadAim=1 - where you look);
    the camera glides smoothly when you transform.
  Grenade (like Half-Life: Alyx): squeeze the grip of the free left hand at your chest or belt - a grenade from
    the "pouch" appears in the hand (light vibration; no grenades - a short click). Left trigger - arm it
    (strong vibration). Release the grip mid-swing - the grenade flies with the hand's speed and direction,
    in an arc. Released without arming it goes back to the pouch (the count is not spent). The Y button throws
    as in the game.
    Settings in [Input]: GrenadeGrab=1 (0 - the old throwing gesture), GrenadeThrowScale (throw strength =
    hand speed x this), GrenadeGravity=1 (the arc; 0 - flies almost straight, as in the game).
  The free hand's fingers copy yours: trigger - index finger (finger off the trigger - pointing), grip - middle
    and ring fingers, the thumb rests while it touches the stick/buttons (raised - "thumbs up"). With a grenade
    or a melee weapon - a fist, on the gun's foregrip - wrapped around it. [VR] FingerPoses=0 - off;
    FingerPalmSign=-1 - if the fingers bend the wrong way.
  Pulling (like the gravity gloves in Alyx/Boneworks): point a free hand (the left; the right one if "Empty
    hand" is picked) at a health pack / ammo / weapon - a light click of vibration - and SQUEEZE THE GRIP: like
    in Boneworks, the item flies smoothly right into your hand (about a second) and is taken. Ammo and health
    count by themselves (if you are short of them); the mod takes weapons itself (holds X; PullAutoTake=0 -
    take with the X button). A weapon rack returns to its place when you walk away.
    Does not work while the left hand holds a melee weapon or the foregrip. [Input]: Pull=1, PullRange=12 (m),
    PullCone=15 (degrees), PullSpeed=0 (above 0 - jerking the hand towards you at that speed, m/s, also pulls).
  Only visible items are pulled (not through walls, PullTrace=1), and only ones not taken yet.
  Interface: only health, abilities, weapon, ammo and button hints ([HUD] Prompts=1) ([HUD] Minimal=1;
    0 - the whole game interface).
  Hints and tutorial pages name the VR controller buttons instead of keys: "Press A to Jump", "Hold X...",
    "R Grip", "L Stick" (left stick click), "Trigger" / "L Trigger". [HUD] ButtonHints=0 - as in the game.
  While the headset is on, the game gets no mouse and keyboard (a mouse bumped by accident does not turn
    the view; BlockMouseKeyboard=0 in [Input] - turn this off).
  The sniper zoom (left trigger with a sniper rifle) is shown in a lens above the barrel.
  In vehicle form the camera is third person by default (VehicleCamera=1, ChaseDistance / ChaseHeight);
    VehicleCamera=0 - from the cockpit (VehicleEyeHeight / VehicleEyeForward).
  Video movies, in-engine cutscenes and menus are on the virtual screen ([VR] Cutscenes3D=1 - cutscenes in 3D
    around you). So are scripted walks where the game's camera follows the characters (the start of Prison Break).
  Interface markers (where to go, names over allies, target lock) are drawn right in the 3D view
    ([HUD] MarkersInEyes); translucent ability effects and text (hints, "reloading") are not - the text stays
    on the panel. The panel with health, abilities and weapon is as before.
  The aim ring shows the game's own crosshair (changes per weapon, turns red on an enemy): [VR] ReticleStyle=1
    (0 - a plain ring). Size and offset - in setup mode. Spread wide (jumping and firing) it stays whole on the
    ring and does not get into the picture.
  Ability animations no longer take over the arms (AbilityAnimation=0). The hand and the gun are always where
    the controller is: the arm stretches at the shoulder, and when it cannot reach - at the forearm too.
  A grenade in the hand does not spin and stays silent until armed; a grenade is visible on the belt while you
    have any (GrenadeBelt); the size of the grenade on the belt and in the hand - GrenadeScale (0.5).
  Sharpness: 16x anisotropic filtering is enabled in BinariesVR\dgVoodoo.conf.

FIX FOR HANGS AND CRASHES
The mod fixes the game hanging at start-up / on window focus changes (a deadlock between dgVoodoo and the engine).
BinariesVR\TWFC.exe is marked Large Address Aware (4 GB of memory instead of 2): without it the VR version
crashed on heavy levels with "You ran out of memory". The original without the flag is BinariesVR\TWFC.exe.noLAA;
Binaries is untouched.

UNINSTALLING
Delete the BinariesVR and BinariesVR_Classic folders, all the "Launch VR*.bat" / "Запуск VR*.bat" files and both
guides. The _vrmod folder is the mod's sources and _vrmod_tools the build tools; they can be deleted too.
